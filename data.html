Straight Talk

What We Won't Tell You

01

We won't quote video stats as gospel

Most "video increases conversions by X%" numbers come from companies selling video software. We looked. The honest answer is that short-form works, the exact lift depends entirely on your business, and anyone quoting you a precise percentage is guessing.

02

We won't show you other people's results

We're new. Posting a screenshot of someone else's account and implying we did it is the oldest trick in this industry, and we're not doing it. The free first month exists so you get your own numbers instead.

03

We won't promise a number we can't control

Nobody can guarantee a ranking or a follower count. What we can commit to is the work actually getting done, on schedule, with a report every month showing exactly what happened.
